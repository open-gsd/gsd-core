**plan-checker no longer passes research questions the researcher marked resolved itself** — Dimension 11 passed on a `(RESOLVED)` heading suffix that the phase researcher could write over its own open questions. Each question RESEARCH.md leaves needing an answer or a ruling, under any heading or in prose, now needs a `D-NN` decision under the phase's CONTEXT.md `<decisions>` that answers it; the checker reports any other question as a blocker. The fix is to cite a decision already on record. Otherwise plan-phase asks the user and records the answer as a `D-NN`, or the user rules in `/gsd-discuss-phase <N>` and re-runs the researcher with `/gsd-plan-phase <N> --research`. No answer is recorded where no one can be asked (`--auto`, or plan-phase running as a background agent, as `/gsd-autonomous` runs it when it does not flatten), where plan-phase stops at the escalation instead and `/gsd-autonomous` is told not to execute the plans of a run that prints that stop, or when the phase has no CONTEXT.md, where the user leaves for `/gsd-discuss-phase <N>`. verify-work's gap-closure check skips the dimension. When an error in an `[ASSUMED]` fact would change the plan, the researcher also lists the question it raises under Open Questions, so such a fact can now stop an unattended run. The researcher is told never to mark its own questions resolved. (#5438)
