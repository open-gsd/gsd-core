**plan-checker no longer passes research questions the researcher marked resolved itself** — Dimension 11 passed on a `(RESOLVED)` heading suffix that the phase researcher could write over its own open questions. Each question RESEARCH.md leaves needing an answer or a ruling, under any heading or in prose, now needs a `D-NN` decision under the phase's CONTEXT.md `<decisions>` that answers it; the checker reports any other question as a blocker. The fix is to cite a decision already on record. Otherwise plan-phase asks the user and records the answer as a `D-NN`, or the user rules in `/gsd-discuss-phase <N>` and re-runs the researcher with `/gsd-plan-phase <N> --research`. No answer is recorded in a pipeline run (`--auto`, `--chain`, or a chained run's `workflow._auto_chain_active`), where plan-phase stops at the escalation instead, or when the phase has no CONTEXT.md, where the user leaves for `/gsd-discuss-phase <N>`. verify-work's gap-closure check skips the dimension. When an error in an `[ASSUMED]` fact would change the plan, the researcher also lists the question it raises under Open Questions, so such a fact can now stop a pipeline run. The researcher is told never to mark its own questions resolved. (#5438)
