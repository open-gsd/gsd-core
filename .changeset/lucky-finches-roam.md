**`/gsd-review` flags an Antigravity review that did not read every plan** — `agy` can exit 0 with a normal-looking review after reading only part of its prompt, or after compacting its context and reviewing from its own summary. The Antigravity lane now asks agy to restate each plan's requirements, wave, depends_on and task count, and stamps its review `[incomplete-read]` (with a `read_check` block in REVIEWS.md) when a restatement is missing or wrong, the output was truncated, or agy compacted its context before writing the review. The restatement is asked for only when the prompt names a plan. Each plan file inside the repository is read once, as a regular file of at most 1 MiB, and at most 8 MiB in all; a plan past those bounds is listed as unchecked and flags the review, as does, when the prompt names a plan, a compaction that could not be ruled out. The marker is an evidence-quality discount marker at the `/gsd-plan-review-convergence` consensus gate, like `[reviewed-without-repo-access]` (#5491).
