**Preflight guarded install destinations before replacing gsd-core/** — the installer checks the gsd-core root, guarded layout artifact destinations, and Codex config.toml and agents root when agent-role generation is enabled. The agents install prunes gsd-* leaves, including TOML symlinks, before the Codex config writer; those leaves retain their writer guard without a new early refusal. Codex installs also preflight hooks.json when projected hook reconciliation is enabled and the scoped migration plan leaves hooks.json and config.toml unchanged; unrelated pending migrations and absence of install state alone do not bypass this check. Planned mutations of those inputs remain an exception: in a migration 002 upgrade containing legacy GSD hooks, the migration removes or replaces the hooks.json symlink without the opt-in, and the reconciler checks the resulting destination after the gsd-core refresh. Caught legacy dev-preferences migration refusals remain outside early-abort preflight: the installer warns, restores the original legacy file, refreshes core, and exits successfully. Refusals caught by preflight preserve the previous install; nested corpus symlinks retain their existing replacement behavior. Artifact plan builders, writers, and applySurface share the config-dir fallback for an empty home; the committed surface resolves before the gsd-core refresh, and orphan recovery runs after destination preflight.
