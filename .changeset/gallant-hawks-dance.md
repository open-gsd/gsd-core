**`state planned-phase` no longer reports success when it recorded no plan count** — a STATE.md whose Current Position holds only `Plan: 0 TBD in current phase` exited 0 with `updated: []` and no count. It now writes `--plans` to the `Plan:` / `Current Plan:` line (a same-phase replan keeps the recorded plan number, the `N of M` spelling and any trailing text, and a bare `N` beside `Total Plans in Phase:` stays as written), moves a bare `Status: Ready` to `Ready to execute`, and fails with a usage error, writing nothing, when `--plans` is not a non-negative integer (`3.5`, `3abc` and `-1` are refused) or is below the recorded plan position (the `N` of `Plan: N of M`, or the bare `N` of `Plan: N` / `Current Plan: N`). With `--plans` it also fails without writing when there is no `Plan:` or `Current Plan:` line under `## Current Position` and no `Total Plans in Phase:` line anywhere (a `Plan:` line outside that section does not count, although `state advance-plan` still reads one). `--plans 0` now reports `0 plans ready` instead of `? plans ready`, and a rerun that changes nothing warns `No fields changed` instead of `transition was a no-op`. Fixes #5320.
