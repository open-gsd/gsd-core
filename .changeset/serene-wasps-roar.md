**`roadmap update-plan-progress` no longer ticks a plan row in a shipped milestone or another phase** — a completed plan's checkbox was ticked at its first match anywhere in ROADMAP.md, so an archived `<details>` milestone or a sibling phase sharing the plan id got the tick instead of the active phase. Rows are now ticked only inside the updated phase's own section and checklist entry (both, when the row is in each) within the active milestone, a `<details>` block after the active milestone no longer stops ticking (nor the Progress-table, Plans-count and completion-checkbox writes), and a phase listed in both the milestone and its `(Phase Details)` window gets its rows inserted and ticked once. A `<details>` block is skipped only when its `<summary>` marks a closed milestone (shipped, archived, ✅), matched by nesting depth, so a notes block or a phase wrapper stays editable and a roadmap with no archived milestone is ticked as before, including rows under a `####` sub-heading of the phase; `-[ ]` lines without a space after the dash are not bullets and are not ticked. `phase complete` shares the milestone scoping, but still ticks plan rows only inside the phase detail section, not checklist-bullet rows. (#5483)
