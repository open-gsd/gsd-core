**The ROADMAP Progress table is now located by one owner** — the progress counters, the state contract and the workstream inventory used to disagree on a `## Progress` heading inside a fenced block and on whether a decoy table could be mistaken for the Progress table; they now share one rule. Two narrow edges also change: the archive-span scan no longer treats a level-1 `# Progress` heading as the Progress section, and a workstream ROADMAP whose milestone table sits outside an existing `## Progress` section is no longer read.
