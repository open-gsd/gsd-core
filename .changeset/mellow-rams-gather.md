**`tdd.review-checkpoint` no longer fails a sealed TDD plan after a squash merge** — a squash merge made a passing TDD plan fail the review checkpoint. Now `--record` seals the evidence, and in a complete history a committed seal credits the plan after the squash, and in a shallow clone it shows a plan with no surviving commit as not evaluable. `ship` commits the seal when the phase's `commit_docs` resolves true (`phase_commit_docs.<phase>` overrides `commit_docs`) and exits 1 before the push when that commit fails. In a complete (non-shallow) history, an unsealed plan with no surviving commit and gone SUMMARY commits shows as not evaluable instead of failing. While the original commits still exist, a plan whose SUMMARY commits were rebased or amended with their subjects intact still fails, when the rewritten commits are not dated before the earliest author or committer time of the original commits. A plan that shows as not evaluable adds a `NotEvaluable` status to the review rows and sets the gate outcome to advisory (was pass). A squash commit titled `<type>(<phase>-<plan>):` with no seal still fails. (#5354)
