**`tdd.review-checkpoint` no longer fails a sealed TDD plan after a squash merge** — a squash merge made a passing TDD plan fail the review checkpoint. Now `--record` seals the evidence. In a complete history, a committed seal credits the plan after the squash. In a shallow clone, a sealed plan with no surviving commit shows as not evaluable; an unsealed one still fails. `ship` commits the seal when the phase's `commit_docs` resolves true (`phase_commit_docs.<phase>` overrides `commit_docs`). It exits 1 before the push when that commit fails. In a complete (non-shallow) history, an unsealed plan with no surviving commit and gone SUMMARY commits shows as not evaluable instead of failing. A `planning.sub_repos` workspace counts as complete when every repo is non-shallow. A plan whose SUMMARY commits were rebased or amended with their subjects intact still fails while the original commits exist. That holds when the rewritten commits are not dated before the earliest author or committer time of the original commits. A plan that shows as not evaluable adds a `NotEvaluable` status to the review rows and sets the gate outcome to advisory (was pass). A squash commit titled `<type>(<phase>-<plan>):` with no seal still fails. (#5354)
